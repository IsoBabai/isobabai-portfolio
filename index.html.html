About Me

I'm Sudipta Das, known in the Web3 space as IsoBabai. Born and raised in India, I entered the crypto and blockchain world in 2020 and never looked back. What started as curiosity became a 6-year journey of building, moderating, and growing some of the most exciting communities in Web3.

I've worked with NFT marketplaces, DeFi protocols, and L2 networks, always focused on bridging the gap between projects and their users across South Asia.

Whether I'm handling a Discord crisis at 2am or organizing a regional AMA, my commitment to the community stays the same show up, be real, build trust.
